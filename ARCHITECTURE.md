# Loadder AI Architecture

## Customer Flow
Intent Selection
→ Business Understanding
→ Business Context
→ Brand DNA / Brand Book
→ Service Recommendation
→ Execution

## Principles
- Customer intent comes before service selection.
- Business Context is the central intelligence layer.
- Existing foundations must be reused.
- Avoid parallel systems.

## Website Builder Direction
Website creation should consume verified business context, not start from empty templates only.
