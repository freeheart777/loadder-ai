# Loadder AI Decisions

## Decision Log

### Intent First Customer Journey
Decision:
Users should not be pushed directly into a service.

Reason:
The platform must understand the business before recommending solutions.

Flow:
Diagnosis / Understanding
→ Recommendation
→ Execution

### Duplicate Systems
Decision:
Do not create duplicate onboarding, business model, or AI workflow systems.

Reason:
Existing foundations remain authoritative.
